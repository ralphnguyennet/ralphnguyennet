**Lost Without Your Love**\

These nights feel longer than before\
This room don’t feel like home no more\
No sound of you, no trace, no light\

I walk the streets we used to know\
But I don’t feel like me no more\
Everything’s here… but something’s gone\
And now I’m trying to hold on

But I’m lost without your face

But it only makes me fall
