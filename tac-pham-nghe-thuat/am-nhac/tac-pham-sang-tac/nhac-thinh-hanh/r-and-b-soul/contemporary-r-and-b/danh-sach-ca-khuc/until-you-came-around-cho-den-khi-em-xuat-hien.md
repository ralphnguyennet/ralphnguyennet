**Cho Đến Khi Em Xuất Hiện**\
**Thể loại:** R\&B / Soul – Contemporary R\&B\
**Tempo:** \~75 BPM (Slow R\&B Groove)\
**Mood:** Ấm áp – Chữa lành – Sâu lắng – Hy vọng\
**Sáng tác:** Nguyễn Hồng Phương

***

**\[Verse 1]**\
Anh đã quen với những đêm dài\

**Until You Came Around**\
**Genre:** R\&B / Soul – Contemporary R\&B\
**Tempo:** \~75 BPM (Slow R\&B Groove)\
**Mood:** Warm – Healing – Emotional – Hopeful\

***

**\[Verse 1]**\
I got used to empty nights\
Walking alone through city lights\
My heart was just an open space\
No one stayed, no one embraced

Thought that love was just a lie\
Something people fantasize\
I hid behind my walls so high\
Too afraid to even try

**\[Outro]**\

You’re the reason… I stay now…
